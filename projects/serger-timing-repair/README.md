# Serger Timing Repair (Brother 1034D)

Diagnose and correct needle/looper timing on a Brother 1034D (or similar) serger — a precision
mechanical adjustment task, not an electronics build, but documented here since it's a real
multi-session repair with tight tolerances worth tracking properly.

## Overview

A serger finishes seams by coordinating a needle bar with an upper and lower looper — all three
must move in exact synchronization, at specific moments, with specific clearances from one
another, to form a proper stitch. When that synchronization ("timing") drifts, symptoms include
skipped stitches, failure to chain, clunking noises, needles striking the loopers or needle plate,
and loose/incomplete stitches. Fixing it means working through a graduated sequence: clean and
oil first (often enough on its own), then work through needle bar height, upper looper timing, and
lower looper timing/position in that order, checking specific clearances at each step with a
digital caliper.

Based on [Fixing the Timing on a Serger — You Can Do It (Ellie & Mac)](https://www.ellieandmac.com/blogs/blog/fixing-the-timing-on-a-serger-you-can-do-it).

## Construction Method / Build Steps

Always hand-crank the machine while testing adjustments — never power it until confident
everything runs smoothly. Unplug from power before cleaning, oiling, or adjusting anything.

1. **Clean** - Remove lint from all crevices with a brush/vacuum attachment; note disassembly
   order for reassembly
2. **Oil** - One drop of sewing machine oil at each metal-on-metal contact point, especially the
   needle bar and gears behind the front cover
3. **Rethread and test** - If it chains properly after cleaning/oiling, done. If not, proceed to
   timing adjustments below
4. **Read the service manual first** - Before touching any timing screw
5. **Needle bar height** - Loosen the #3 hex screw at the top of the needle bar; target 11.3-11.9mm
   clearance between needle tip and plate at the needle's highest point; keep needles parallel to
   the plate
6. **Upper looper timing** - Remove front cover (#2 hex wrench on the fixing screws); looper should
   sit centered between the two needles at their highest point, ~1mm clearance; when the left
   needle is at its extreme-left position, maintain 5.6-6.0mm looper-to-needle clearance
7. **Lower looper position** - Loosen the hexagonal screw at the looper base (8mm wrench); target
   2.0-2.4mm clearance between needle plate and looper tip at the looper's highest point, and
   12.5mm from the lower looper point to the left needle center
8. **Lower looper timing** - With needles raised 2.2-2.6mm above their lowest position and the
   lower looper contacting the needle's right side: 0-0.05mm looper-to-needle clearance, 1.2-1.5mm
   looper-to-moveable-guard clearance, 0-0.1mm needle-to-moveable-guard, 0-0.05mm needle-to-fixed-guard
9. **Verify looper coordination** - Upper and lower loopers should pass each other "like puzzle
   pieces" with no collision; if they don't interlock cleanly, expect skipped stitches and loose
   tension

## Key Features

- Fully mechanical fix — no parts replacement required if the issue is pure timing drift
- Graduated approach (clean/oil first) avoids unnecessary teardown if that alone resolves it
- Every adjustment has an explicit tolerance from the source guide, not guesswork
